Custom rules

One rule per line. Lines starting with ! are comments. Network rules such as ||ads.example.com^ and element hiding such as example.com##.banner{' '} are supported.

Statistics

Counts come from Chrome's rule-match debug event, which is only dispatched to unpacked extensions. They are stored locally and never leave this device.

About

QuietBlock is a personal ad blocker. It blocks advertising and tracking requests with Chrome's declarativeNetRequest API, hides advertising elements with a stylesheet injected before the page renders, and keeps its filter lists on your machine.

No telemetry, no analytics, no promotional pages, no donation prompts. The extension never opens a tab on its own.
